This is a simple Twitter Bootstrap 4 template. To check BS4 and Bootstrap-table compatibility.

## html

```html
<script src="//labo.infocapagde.com/lib/bootstrap-4.0.0-alpha/js/bootstrap.js"></script>
<link
  rel="stylesheet"
  href="//labo.infocapagde.com/lib/bootstrap-4.0.0-alpha/css/bootstrap.css"
/>
<script src="//code.jquery.com/ui/1.11.4/jquery-ui.min.js"></script>
<link
  rel="stylesheet"
  href="//ajax.googleapis.com/ajax/libs/jqueryui/1.11.4/themes/smoothness/jquery-ui.css"
/>
<input type="text" id="datepicker" />
```
