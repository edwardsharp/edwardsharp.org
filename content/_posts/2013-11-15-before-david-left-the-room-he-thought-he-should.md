> Before David left the room, he thought he should say some words to his friend. If there was a limbo or a bardo, he was there now and might be afraid, might be confused, might need reassurance. “But nothing comes from my mouth,” David wrote. “This is the most important event of my life and my mouth can’t for words and maybe I’m the one who needs words, may I’m the one who needs reassurance and all I can do is raise my hands from my hands from my sides in helplessness and say, ‘All I want is some sort of grace.’” Then David began to cry.

—FIRE INTHEBELLY
