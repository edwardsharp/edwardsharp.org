## zomg dance

zomg church [Malcolm-X El-Shabazz Betts, Anna Nowicka, Robin Prichard, Katarzyna Sitarz]

pre-talk with E: what about potentiality? so like, in performance there are objectz that have some sort of intrinsic (maybe sentience, mostly my own historical baggage) quality. these objectz’ potential can be engaged, duh, but there’s something (well a lot, for me) to the resistance to/of engaging this potential. E suggested that theatricalization was a good name for the process of bringing to light the potential these objectz possess. which can, well, set the stage for an enthralling dynamic between human body & object. E seyz: semiotics, duh. but i think semiotics is perhaps a more abstract (abstract here in adjective form) concept; these objectz i’m talking about are indeed real and physical and present and event sometime concrete (like made out of fucking cement). okay… so what i want to figure out is how i qualify potential. i mean, i get that preference and taste are substantial factorz. for example: modular synthesizerz; i’m technical as fuck so i grok a lot of what’s going on but i’m also super turned off by bourgeoisie (uh, white) culture. so in the case of modular synthz i tend to recognize a lot of potential, cuz like, i’ve worked with them and i can see there’s a lot of wirez to plug and unplug and there’s a ton of knobz to twist. but i know there’s a lot of shit in all that potential that i don’t want to hear, like, at all. so, there’s resistance to twisting every knob. nobody probably wantz to sit around while every permutation is exhausted (and maybe it might take more than an average human lifetime?).

ugh, so bla bla virtuosity (fuck) so bla bla craft… the example of sound/music/noise sortof has to stop here.

what about a concrete sculpture that a person bringz into a performance? it’s covered, shroud quite explicitly. becoming un-shroud but never being un-shroud. me wanting (i guess) the concrete sculpture to become not-covered. or rather, i quantify/qualify/objectify this action as potential. and when the performer doesn’t uncover the concrete sculpture i’m pleased.

all this to say, i guess, that i like it when performerz don’t do the (obvious?) thing that i think they could/should/might do. hmm.

…then it happened. d00d really considered the aestheticz of movement & twisting knobz. i’m overjoy’d.
