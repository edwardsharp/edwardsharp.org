<p>Conventional documentaries, like most cinematic narratives, exist within rigid and specialized <br>
frameworks comprised of delicately balanced audio and visual components. Although they allude <br>
to something real, they ultimately exist as products of a creative endeavor. Towards Omniscient <br>
Documentary is an ongoing project that serves as my vehicle to explore this paradoxical nature of <br>
documentary. By building expanded audiovisual environments that recount 'true' stories, it is my <br>
goal to shed light  on  the  real-est of real realms—the subjective, hybrid space that exists <br>
somewhere in between objective reality, and personal interpretation</p>
