# well

are we gonna do it, or not?

either/or?

heh, but like, wut time would u listen to elliot smith?

like, what time of day.

let's go there.

bring it on

i've been practicing

make an imprint on my memory

i've been practicing how to remember

no hostility

i'll tell yr story in the best way

we've got the best reinterpretation

trust me

$me

do or do not overlook the price of a gate

we all gotta walk thru them

they are necessary

yr necessary

blood stainz

my blood

blood stainz
