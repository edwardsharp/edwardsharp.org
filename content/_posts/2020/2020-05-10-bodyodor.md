# bodyodor

a poor copy

or like: lolol, poor copy

what even is a dataset

but a reason to reason about

to rescind or otherwise

play some little game

keep it down for the night

swelling abscess

cramped viscera

take a walk in the street

forget to water the planetz

the way this was designed

is not to sustain

the explanation needed

was not taken into account

now take a walk in the park

go out every day

maggot death

let's go all the way down

and look at tomorrow's daylight

let's slow down

remain viscous

thingz take time, y'kno?

what even is an ontology

well, let's see

handy little freak

stay the fuck away from me
