# where do we go from here

**# adventive**

but if i were to have believed

how much power i had

when i came to be

well, there'd be little left to eat

for all we know, well coulda knew well not to

but then i'm placed back, here

and forget becoming

i am old, and the skin hangin from these bonez

isn't getting me any more

but they could

i'll show you

i'll show u all

# \*\*naturalized\*\*

i think maybe that's how i'll go

deep

i'll find that one k-line and over-play it

over and over and over

until it's real

and then i get what i want

and then, well

i just want to see all destruction for as long as i like

but then put it all back

undo or whatever it takes to make it not-permanent

go deeper and deeper (more or less personnel)

and how it feelz like real when any minor key is bearable

after a while i get used to it

and have new seedz to trade

my barren landscape is

for the taking here

the crop needs to be taken

the fruit

the seed

the cycle

this thing i call need

the species is feral

i don't know why

but they say it's true

and some talk about dismantle'n

(which is mostly code)

caught young-of-the-year crappiezindicate the adultz found suitable

spawning habitat on the floodplain

stratification can be obvious

post-glacial rebound

the esker was a sign that

there was a river underneath the ice

now it's just some fucking hiking path

not sure if it's the time, now, or not

but the soft massage will have to do, for now

until i can show it all

to you

but for now \<\<...>>
