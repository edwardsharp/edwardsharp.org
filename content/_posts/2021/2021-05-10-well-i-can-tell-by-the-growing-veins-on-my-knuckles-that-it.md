# well i can tell by the growing veins on my knuckles that it's my turn to go

well i can see by the look on the table

well i see you have not got any liquid left

your cavity is itching

but what to put inside

or expose the outside

to just rub against

or pass through

why do i care which?

elbow grease, let's talk about that.

it adds up.

to what quantity
