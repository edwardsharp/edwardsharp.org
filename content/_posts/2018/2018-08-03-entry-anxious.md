an xious

where to contain my own \_\_\_\_\_\_\_

and then find suitable disposal

and a suitable suitor

maybe time travel existz like when you listen to that album from a decade ago

but we don’t have wordz for what we are calling time travel

probably best not to think of it like travel, anyway

i guess what i’m wanting to do is to stop scarring this timeline

not like, neutrality but like, self-induced coma for 1-10 yearz

but somehow without the debt attached to 1-10 yearz

or maybe i should just call it death

and then give another a chance a birth

but i could just embrace my own rebirth

and then reinvent

but it’s all to real; the here & now

what is there to know about rhythm

i just try and listen

to it

for it

with it

and somehow i know when

the time is right

and that’s not right now

recently freaked out about something i threw away a while ago

like, did i fuck up

and it doesn’t even matter cuz all that’s gone now

but not really 1. i might have picz 2. i still remember it 3. and it probably hasn’t decomposed yet

self-induced drudgery

pull off the slag

lick it all off

ow as in ouch

wanna use all the stuff outta the tube

love in a tube lookz like |\<3\<3\<3==
