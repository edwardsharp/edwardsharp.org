HOW & WHO; the labour of ‘making it happen’.

**how** to be a governing body that ‘acceptz’ rather than ‘selectz’?

**who** to trust with our attention?

_“The curator’s job has been, among other things, to find the through line. She provides a practical function, telling us who to trust with our attention; moreover, she fulfills an emotional-psychological one, by making sense of a world overrun with entities and ideas. But once curators themselves proliferate, and the curators need curating, and the notion of “curation” becomes so romantic that dilettantes assume the pose until it’s commonplace – then everyone and no one is a curator, and curation is everything and nothing, and nothing makes sense again. What happens when there are no more arbiters of taste, no rank of persons who, through instinct, experience and training, know best?”_

_–**The Rise of the Curator in Popular Culture**, Alexandra Molotkow_

topics: collaborative curation; self-organization;

the labour: navigating ethics of curation. finding artistz. engaging with the ~27,000 people in 1 square mile vicinity; this is too much, so how are we particular? the labour of making a list or many many listz; who’s on those listz & how did they get there? if the list is published will it invigorate people (on the list, or not) to make performance(z)? the craft(ing) of an open call. how do individualz and groupz craft such a call? is it possible to set aside space for trying to improve the language of the call -or- does this just tase time?

very very important question: **HOW** DO PEOPLE BENEFIT? and related: **how** can people (or rather, myself) provide something beneficial?

if there are _limited resources,_ it’s important to carefully divide them. **how** does the perspective of resource quantities affect responsibility? can these perspectivez be changed or shifted, and if so, what are the consequences?

ideation <==> pragmatism (or, how do ideaz get activated?)

doing nothing <==> affecting change (is doing nothing important?)
