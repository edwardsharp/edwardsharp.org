The Fold & Aether Art Projects present UNFOLDING, an immersive night of performance by artists from New York, Baltimore, DC, and more. To unfold is to open up, to spread out and expand. UNFOLDING is about sharing and revealing through actions and movements.

Featured artists include Panoply Performance Lab, Future Death Toll, Carolyn Becker, Ziad Nagy, Renée Regan, Eames Armstrong, Eve Henessa, Rachel Hrbek, Ian McDermott, Natalia Panfile, Michael Gagnon and more to be announced.
