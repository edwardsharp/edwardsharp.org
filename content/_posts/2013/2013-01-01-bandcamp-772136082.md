<p>These sounds are recordings from a Pure Data patch that sequences several tones and midi notes at varying pitch and rate. The MIDI output data can be fed into a variety of software or hardware instruments. The patch can output sounds on its own and thus can act as an instrument in its own right. This patch and audio recordings are released to the creative commons in the hopes that other humans can find any or all of these things useful in their own course of being creative. <br>
